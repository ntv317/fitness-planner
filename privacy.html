Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Fitness Planner does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • Everything stays on your device. Your exercises, routines, workout logs and photos are saved only in the app's local storage on your iPhone. We have no servers and no account system.
  • No uploads. Fitness Planner never sends your data anywhere. "Plan with AI" only builds a text prompt that you choose to copy into another app yourself.
  • Watch Demo links. Tapping "Demo" on an exercise opens a YouTube search for that exercise's name in Safari or the YouTube app. Only the exercise name is included in that search; it is then handled by YouTube under Google's privacy policy. Nothing else leaves the app.
  • No tracking, analytics or ads. The app contains no third-party SDKs.
  • Camera and photos. If you add exercise photos, the camera or photo picker is used only to attach that image to your exercise, on your device.
  • Notifications. Rest-timer alerts are local notifications scheduled on your device.
  • Contacting support. If you email us from the app, your email app sends the message you write, plus the app and iOS version, to us. We use it only to answer you and do not share it.
  • Deleting your data. Deleting the app removes all of its data.
