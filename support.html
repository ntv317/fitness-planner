Common questions

Where is my data stored? Can I back it up?

Everything is stored on your iPhone. It is included in your normal iPhone or iCloud device backup, so restoring a backup to a new phone brings your workouts with it.

How do I get started quickly?

On the welcome screen or the empty Routines tab, tap "Load Sample Plan" for a ready-made 3-day full-body plan. Then open Today and start the next workout.

Plan with AI didn't import my plan.

Paste the AI's full reply, including the { } braces. If it still fails, ask the AI to reply with only the JSON from the prompt and paste again.

I don't get an alert when rest is over.

Open Settings in Fitness Planner and tap "Allow Rest Alerts", or turn on notifications for Fitness Planner in the iPhone Settings app.

How do I switch between kg and lb?

Settings → Weight unit. Weights are stored exactly; switching only changes how they're shown.

How do I delete all my data?

Delete the app from your iPhone. This removes all of its data.
